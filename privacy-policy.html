2. البيانات التي تبقى على جهازك

البيانات التي تدخلها تُحفظ في مساحة التطبيق الخاصة على جهازك، وتشمل:

  • بيانات الجمعية: الاسم، اسم المدير، المبلغ الشهري، عدد الأشهر والأسماء، تاريخ البداية.
  • بيانات الأعضاء التي يدخلها المدير: الاسم، العنوان، وسيلة التواصل، عدد الأسماء.
  • ترتيب الأدوار وسجل التحصيل والخصم والتسليم.
  • إعدادات التذكير.

لا تغادر هذه البيانات جهازك إلا إذا فعّلت المشاركة، أو حفظت نسخة احتياطية بنفسك.

3. النسخ الاحتياطي

  • نحتفظ تلقائيا على جهازك بآخر 7 نسخ يومية داخل مساحة التطبيق، وتُحذف عند حذف التطبيق.
  • عند اختيارك «حفظ نسخة احتياطية» يُحفظ الملف في المكان الذي تختاره (الجهاز أو Google Drive أو غيره)، وتحكمه سياسة ذلك المكان.
  • ملف النسخة الاحتياطية غير مشفّر، فاحفظه في مكان آمن ولا تشاركه مع من لا تثق به.

4. المشاركة السحابية (اختيارية)

إذا ضغط المدير «نشر الجمعية» نرفع إلى الخادم، لكل عضو على حدة:

  • كود الانضمام الخاص به، ومعرّف الجمعية ومعرّف العضو.
  • اسم العضو، واسم الجمعية، واسم المدير، والمبلغ الشهري، وعدد الأشهر والأسماء، وتاريخ البداية.
  • حصة العضو، وحالة دفعه لكل شهر، وموعد قبضه ومبلغه وحالة تسليمه.

لا نرفع عناوين الأعضاء ولا وسائل التواصل الخاصة بهم، ولا بيانات الأعضاء الآخرين. كل عضو يرى بياناته هو فقط.

عندما يربط العضو الكود تُحفظ على جهازه نسخة للقراءة فقط. وعندما يبلّغ بالدفع يُسجَّل بلاغ (الشهر والحالة والوقت) يراه المدير ليؤكده أو يرفضه.

5. معرّف الجهاز المجهول

لاستخدام المشاركة ينشئ التطبيق تلقائيا هوية مجهولة عشوائية لجهازك لدى مزوّد الخدمة السحابية، دون بريد إلكتروني أو رقم هاتف أو اسم. تُحفظ مفاتيح هذه الهوية على جهازك، وتُستخدم فقط لتحديد صاحب الكود ومن يحق له القراءة والتعديل.

حذف بيانات التطبيق قد يفقدك صلاحية تعديل الجمعيات التي نشرتها أو الجمعيات المربوطة بحسابك، وحينها يلزم نشر جديد أو كود جديد.

9. الاحتفاظ والحذف

  • على جهازك: حتى تحذفها أو تحذف التطبيق.
  • على الخادم: حتى يوقف المدير المشاركة أو يحذف الجمعية أو يولّد كودا جديدا للعضو، وعندها تُحذف السجلات المرتبطة. قد تبقى سجلات تقنية قصيرة (مثل عدد محاولات إدخال الأكواد) لأغراض الحماية.

Privacy Policy — ElGam3ya

1. Introduction

“ElGam3ya” helps you organise monthly savings groups (“gam'eya”): manage members, the order of turns, record collections and payouts, and follow your participation as a member.

It is provided by ThawbaLabs . Effective date: 27 أكتوبر 2026 / October 27, 2026.

The app works fully on your device with no account. Cloud sharing is optional.

2. Data kept on your device

Data you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device, including:

  • Group details: name, manager name, monthly amount, number of months and names, start date.
  • Member details entered by the manager: name, address, contact method, number of shares.
  • The order of turns and the ledger of collections, deductions and payouts.
  • Reminder settings.

This data does not leave your device unless you turn on sharing or save a backup yourself.

3. Backups

  • The app automatically keeps the last 7 daily copies inside its private storage on your device; they are deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • When you choose “Save a backup”, the file is saved wherever you choose (device, Google Drive, etc.) and is governed by that location's own policy.
  • Backup files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe and do not share them with people you do not trust.

4. Cloud sharing (optional)

When a manager taps “Publish”, we upload to our server, separately for each member:

  • That member's join code, and the group and member identifiers.
  • The member's name, the group name and manager name, the monthly amount, number of months and names, and the start date.
  • The member's share, payment status for each month, and the month, amount and delivery status of their payout.

We do not upload members' addresses or contact details, nor other members' data. Each member sees only their own data.

When a member links a code, a read-only copy is kept on their device. When they report a payment, a report (month, status, time) is recorded for the manager to confirm or reject.

5. Anonymous device identity

To use sharing, the app automatically creates a random anonymous identity for your device with our cloud provider, with no email, phone number or name. Its keys are stored on your device and used only to determine who owns a code and who may read or edit.

Clearing the app's data may cost you the ability to edit groups you published or access groups you linked; you would then need to publish again or get a new code.

6. What we do not collect

We do not directly collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or photos.

The app contains ads (banner and full-screen interstitial) provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The advertising provider may process technical information such as the advertising ID, device information, IP address, and ad interaction information for ad delivery and measurement, subject to device settings and Google's policies. Personalized ads may be used where permitted by applicable law and platform settings, and ads may vary by region and user settings.

We do not sell or rent users' data, and we do not use your association data for advertising. Core association features do not depend on ads.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: only for sharing, linking and payment reports. The rest of the app works without it.
  • Notifications: to show reminders about collection, payment and payout dates; they are scheduled on your device with no server.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the device restarts.

8. Cloud provider

We use Supabase to host the optional sharing data. It is stored in a data centre in West Europe (Ireland) and transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The provider acts as an infrastructure provider, and your data is also subject to its terms and policy.

9. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • On the server: until the manager stops sharing, deletes the group, or generates a new code for a member; the related records are then deleted. Short technical logs (such as the number of code attempts) may remain for protection purposes.
  • When a member unlinks, only the copy on their device is deleted. To request deletion of your data on the server, ask the group manager or contact us.

10. Security

We use transport encryption, server-side access rules that prevent any member from reading another member's data, and a local financial ledger chained with digital fingerprints to detect tampering.

No security is absolute; you are responsible for protecting your device (screen lock), join codes and backups.

11. Managers and other people's data

If you are a group manager and enter members' data (names, addresses, contact details), you are responsible for having their consent and for using the data only to run the group.

12. Children

The app is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete your data inside the app at any time, and you may ask us about or request deletion of your data on the server at: thawbalab@gmail.com.

14. Changes and contact
